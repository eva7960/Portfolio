Hello I'm Eva!

I graduated from the University of Washington in March 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a Math minor. My coursework was largely backend-focused, so I assumed I'd become a Java developer.

I was filling out a job application for a software engineer position, and I was asked to describe an experience that shaped who I am. I've worked in the restaurant industry since I was 17 to put myself through college, so I started typing the usual answer, how I learned time management, communication, and love of working with people. But as I wrote, something clicked.

I started describing how I'd memorized the regulars — the man who always settled into the Victorian chairs with his book, the family of four that came in every weekend. How I'd learned each server's strengths, figured out who could handle a big party and who needed more time before being sat. I loved the feeling of orchestrating the flow of the whole restaurant, and how the high-volume nights felt like a fun challenge instead of a stressor. After 2 years, I was promoted from host to server at 19, and now, after 3 years of serving, I can walk in and immediately sense where the team needs support — whether that's running food, refilling waters, or grabbing ice for a slammed bartender. I thrive when I'm helping people, and I thrive when things are moving fast. Halfway through writing that answer, I stopped and closed the application.

I realized I didn't want to be strictly behind a screen writing backend code. I wanted a role where I could also work directly with people, understand what they need, and help deliver something meaningful.

If that kind of passion is what you're looking for in a teammate, I'd love to connect, you can find my email, LinkedIn, and GitHub below.
